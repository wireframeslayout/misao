# Contributing to misao

Thanks for your interest in misao.

- misao is pre-alpha. The socket protocol may change without notice until `@misao/protocol` 1.0.
- Open an issue before starting a large change, so the design can be agreed first.
- Commit messages follow [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/)
  (`feat:`, `fix:`, `refactor:`, `docs:`, `test:`, `chore:`).
- On your first pull request, the CLA bot asks you to agree to the [CLA](CLA.md) by posting a
  comment. The project is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE).
